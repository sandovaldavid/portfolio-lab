I. Introduction & Motivation

Traditional time-series predictors assume linear dependencies or fail to map long-range temporal dependencies due to numerical instability. Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs) theoretically address this, yet the exponential product of Jacobians during backpropagation through time yields vanishing or exploding gradients. Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) cells mitigate this by introducing linear cell state updates, governed by specialized multiplicative gates. This research proposal aims to build on top of these foundations by optimizing memory retention on high-frequency systems.
